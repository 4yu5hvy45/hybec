Frequently asked questions

01Do you offer lighting consultation?

Yes. From understanding your requirements to recommending the right solutions, HYBEC works with architects, interior designers and homeowners through every stage of a project.

02Can fixtures be customised?

Body colour, light colour, beam angle and reflector finish can be specified to suit the design, along with trimless or non-trimless options on selected downlights.

03Which colour temperature should I choose?

2700K reads warm and intimate for homes and hospitality, 3000K is a balanced warm white, and 4000K suits offices and retail. Our consultants can mock up options on site.

04Do you make outdoor and façade lighting?

Yes: bollards, in-ground, step and façade lighting designed for landscapes, walkways, driveways and hotel exteriors.

05Where can I see HYBEC in person?

Visit our experience centre or meet us across the design calendar, including ACETECH and FOAID in Mumbai and New Delhi.
